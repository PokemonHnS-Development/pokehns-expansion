# PokeGear RIBBONS (src/pokenav_ribbons_list.c, src/pokenav_ribbons_summary.c).
# Ladder 6 has FLAG_SYS_RIBBON_GET and the Champion Ribbon on the party (Hall of Fame). Here:
#  - party slot 6 becomes a Staraptor given by this ROM (its nickname is the ROM's species name, so
#    the Hebrew run shows a Hebrew name in the list; the longest Hebrew species name, 57 px)
#  - the party gets the Land/Marine/Sky/National/Earth/World/Artist ribbons, the lead the Effort
#    Ribbon (src/field_specials.c:1505-1515, :5973-6160)
#  - party mon 1 gets all 20 contest ribbons, mon 2 four (GiveMonContestRibbon,
#    src/contest_util.c:1983: rank by rank, needs gContestFinalStandings[player] == 0)
#  - gift-ribbon slots get descriptions (gSaveBlock1Ptr->giftRibbons, read by
#    PrintRibbonNameAndDescription, src/pokenav_ribbons_summary.c:834-850)
#  - the six party mons are cloned into box 14, so the list holds 12 mons and scrolls
