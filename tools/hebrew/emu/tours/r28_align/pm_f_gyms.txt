# Mid-game play from ladder save 3: gyms. The Ecruteak Gym after the badge (the guide, a
# statue, Morty), the Olivine Gym while Jasmine is away (the guide, a statue), then the
# Cianwood Gym: Chuck's intro (he smashes the rocks), the leader battle, the badge with its
# fanfare, the badge speech, the TM and its speech. The battle is shortened by setting the
# foe's HP low before each attack (sethp), with the RNG pinned so both ROMs see the same
# battle. sethp writes the party and only battler 0's gBattleMons copy, so the active foe's
# HP is written directly (struct BattlePokemon 0x88 bytes, hp at +0x2A).
start saves/ladder3_ecruteak.sav
include ../../scripts/continue.txt

# --- Ecruteak Gym (beaten): the guide at (12,44), a statue at (13,44), Morty at (4,4)
warpto MAP_ECRUTEAK_CITY_GYM_HNS 12 45
face UP
talk pmf_ecru_guide 10
warpto MAP_ECRUTEAK_CITY_GYM_HNS 13 45
face UP
talk pmf_ecru_statue 10
warpto MAP_ECRUTEAK_CITY_GYM_HNS 4 5
face UP
talk pmf_ecru_morty 10

# --- Olivine Gym: the guide at (10,18) faces down, a statue at (11,18)
warpto MAP_OLIVINE_CITY_HNS 11 28
snap pmf_popup_olivine
warpto MAP_OLIVINE_CITY_GYM_HNS 10 19
face UP
talk pmf_oliv_guide 20
warpto MAP_OLIVINE_CITY_GYM_HNS 11 19
face UP
talk pmf_oliv_statue 10

# --- Cianwood Gym: Chuck at (10,2) between two rocks
warpto MAP_CIANWOOD_CITY_HNS 15 45
snap pmf_popup_cianwood
warpto MAP_CIANWOOD_GYM_HNS 7 15
face UP
talk pmf_cian_statue 10
fastbattles off
warpto MAP_CIANWOOD_GYM_HNS 10 3
face UP
pinrng
talk pmf_chuck_intro 20 A
waitbattle 1800
sethp enemy 0 1
sethp enemy 1 1
sethp enemy 2 1
sethp enemy 3 1
bwalk pmf_chuck_b1 input:HandleInputChooseAction
snap pmf_chuck_action
baction FIGHT
waitbinput HandleInputChooseMove
snap pmf_chuck_moves
bmove 3                                   # Swift (moves: Mud-Slap, Defense Curl, Rollout, Swift)
bwalk pmf_chuck_t1 input:HandleInputChooseAction
w16 @gBattleMons+B2@ 1                  # the active foe (gBattleMons[1].hp) at 1 HP
sethp player 0 79
baction FIGHT
bmove 3
bwalk pmf_chuck_t2 prompt:Cmd_yesnobox
snap pmf_chuck_switch_q
tap B 4                                   # NO: keep Quilava in
bwalk pmf_chuck_t2b input:HandleInputChooseAction
w16 @gBattleMons+B2@ 1                  # the active foe (gBattleMons[1].hp) at 1 HP
sethp player 0 79
baction FIGHT
bmove 3
bwalk pmf_chuck_t3 input:HandleInputChooseAction
w16 @gBattleMons+B2@ 1                  # the active foe (gBattleMons[1].hp) at 1 HP
sethp player 0 79
baction FIGHT
bmove 3
bwalk pmf_chuck_t4 prompt:Cmd_yesnobox
tap B 4
bwalk pmf_chuck_t4b input:HandleInputChooseAction
w16 @gBattleMons+B2@ 1                  # the active foe (gBattleMons[1].hp) at 1 HP
sethp player 0 79
baction FIGHT
bmove 3
bwalk pmf_chuck_t5 prompt:Cmd_yesnoboxlearnmove
snap pmf_chuck_learn_q                    # Togepi (Exp. Share) grew a level: forget a move?
tap B 4                                   # NO: it does not learn the move
bwalk pmf_chuck_t5b prompt:Cmd_yesnobox
tap B 4                                   # NO: no switch
bwalk pmf_chuck_t5c input:HandleInputChooseAction
w16 @gBattleMons+B2@ 1                  # the active foe (gBattleMons[1].hp) at 1 HP
sethp player 0 79
baction FIGHT
bmove 3
bwalk pmf_chuck_win
