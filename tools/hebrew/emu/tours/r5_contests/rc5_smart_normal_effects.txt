# Party slot 0 becomes a level-50 BRONZONG made by this ROM (so its nickname is the ROM's species name)
# with the moves below; the appeals are the digit string 01002 (the move-list index for each round):
#   0 MAGIC_ROOM (SMART, CONTEST_EFFECT_DONT_EXCITE_AUDIENCE, src/data/moves_info.h)
#   1 PRISMATIC_LASER (SMART, CONTEST_EFFECT_JAMS_OTHERS_BUT_MISS_ONE_TURN, src/data/moves_info.h)

#   3 DISABLE (SMART, CONTEST_EFFECT_MAKE_FOLLOWING_MONS_NERVOUS, src/data/moves_info.h)
# Magic Room (don't excite the audience: the crowd keeps watching it, the next appeals are ignored), Prismatic Laser (jams, can't appeal next turn: round 3 is skipped), Magic Room again, Hypnosis.

# audience are the same on the Hebrew and the English ROM (the shots pair up by name).
