# Exp. Candy L on a low-level Pokemon (src/party_menu.c ItemUseCB_RareCandy, then
# Task_TryLearnNewMoves :6026 and PartyMenuTryEvolution :6094): Cyndaquil Lv 12 gains
# 10000 Exp. (973 -> 10973, Lv 24), the level-up stats pages, the move prompt for Quick
# Attack (Lv 13; the other moves up to Lv 24 are already known), YES, forget Smokescreen
# ("1, 2 and... Poof!"), then the evolution into Quilava (Lv 14).
# Cyndaquil's gen-7 learnset (P_LVL_UP_LEARNSETS GEN_7): 12 Smokescreen, 13 Quick Attack,
# 19 Flame Wheel, 20 Mud Shot, 22 Defense Curl. MEDICINE: 6 L, 7 XL (scroll 0).
start saves/pif2_base.sav

w8 @gBagPosition+5@ 1
w16 @gBagPosition+A@ 6
w16 @gBagPosition+16@ 0
startmenu BAG
waitcb2x CB2_BagMenuRun 600
waittaskx Task_BagMenu_HandleInput 600
waitquiet
snap pbi_xce_01_bag_l

waittaskx Task_ItemContext_MultipleRows 60
waitquiet
tap A 4
waitcb2x CB2_UpdatePartyMenu 900
waittaskx Task_HandleChooseMonInput 600

waitquiet

tap A 4

snap pbi_xce_04_stats_pg1
tap A 4
waittaskx Task_TryLearnNewMoves 600
snap pbi_xce_05_stats_pg2
tap A 4

snap pbi_xce_07_which

waitquiet

waitquiet

waitcb2x CB2_Overworld 600
