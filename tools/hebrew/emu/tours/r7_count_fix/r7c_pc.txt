# Count lines in the PC item storage (DECISIONS.md Q125, src/player_pc.c) and the bag's DEPOSIT
# mode (src/item_menu.c Task_ItemContext_Deposit): TOSS (how many, the YES/NO with the count, the
# report) on BIG BAMBOO SHOOT, WITHDRAW on ODD KEYSTONE, DEPOSIT on FOSSILIZED DINO, each all 999
# (the widest count) and each among the widest plurals. The PC's first two slots (POTION and
