# The Battle Pyramid bag's TOSS lines (src/battle_pyramid_bag.c BagAction_Toss, AskConfirmToss,
# TossItem), which share the bag's strings and, under TEXT_RTL, now take the plural name too
# (DECISIONS.md Q125). The bag is opened in its toss mode with special
# ChooseItemsToTossFromPyramidBag (the screen a won run shows when the bag is full), on two
# items written into SaveBlock2.frontier.pyramidBag (+0xE2C: u16 itemId[2][10], then u8
# quantity[2][10]; both level modes): MAX ELIXIR x99 (one of the widest plurals in the medicine
# pool, the largest Pyramid count) and POTION x5.

w8 @SB2+E5E@ 63                           # quantity [1][0]
w8 @SB2+E5F@ 5                            # quantity [1][1]
