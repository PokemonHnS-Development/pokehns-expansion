# Fixed Easy Chat phrases of Frontier and Battle Tent trainers: the intro speech each prints before
# its battle, in the field message box, through the game's own path: the trainer is written into
# gTrainerBattleParameter.opponentA (+2, struct TrainerBattleParameter), then tower_getopponentintro
# (BATTLE_TOWER_FUNC_GET_OPPONENT_INTRO: SetFacilityPtrsGetLevel, FrontierSpeechToString of
# speechBefore, src/battle_tower.c) and msgbox gStringVar4, as the Battle Tower battle room does.
# Frontier trainers with frontier.lvlMode 0 (gBattleFrontierTrainers); tent trainers with lvlMode
# 2 (FRONTIER_LVL_TENT) and VAR_FRONTIER_FACILITY naming the tent (SetTentPtrsGetLevel: FACTORY =
# Slateport, PALACE = Verdanturf, ARENA = Fallarbor), both restored afterwards. The same trainers'
# win and loss speeches are shown in a battle by the battle-string sweep with _bs_phrases_*.
# Run: scripts/r18_easy_chat/ec18.py tour ROM OUT --tours tools/hebrew/emu/tours/r18_easychat --only '^ec_phrases$'
