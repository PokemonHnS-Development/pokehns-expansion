# The Odd Egg clerk (Route34_DayCare_hns/scripts.inc) given each of the 11 answers in turn
# (sInterviewPasswords, src/easy_chat.c; the egg for answer N is sOddEggSpecies[N], src/scrcmd.c).
# First the four bookshelves: HELP_DAYCARE1..4 (src/data/help_window.h) give, for Pichu to Magby, the
# ability, the type, the cry and the habit, each as the word of the Easy Chat list to pick. The
# answers below are the words those hints name (one word a hint: abilities and types from STATUS,
# cries from VOICES, habits from ACTIONS, Pichu's PLAY from FEELINGS). Mantyke, Bonsly, Happiny and
# Mime Jr. have no hint window; their answers are the table's.
# For each answer: a party of two (the egg goes to slot 2), the flag cleared, the clerk's question
# pages, the four words through the keyboard and the lists, OK -> YES, "take care" and the fanfare
# line, then FLAG_RECEIVED_ODD_EGG set and slot 2 an egg of the expected species (eggcheck).
# Run: scripts/r18_easy_chat/ec18.py tour ROM OUT --tours tools/hebrew/emu/tours/r18_easychat --only '^ec_oddegg_all$'
start saves/ladder6_postgame.sav

face UP
