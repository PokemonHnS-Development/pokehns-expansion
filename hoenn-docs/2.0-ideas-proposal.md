## 1. Cutting post-Gen-3 Pokémon

### What's measured
pokeemerald-expansion has a switch per generation (`P_GEN_4_POKEMON` … `P_GEN_9_POKEMON` in `include/config/species_enabled.h`) and one per family. Families are filed under their **oldest** member's generation, and cross-generation evolutions (Magnezone, Weavile, Togekiss, Munchlax, Ursaluna…) stay on through `P_CROSS_GENERATION_EVOS`. So turning gens 4–9 off is exactly "keep Gen 1–3 and their evolutions". Regional forms of Gen 1–3 species (Alolan Vulpix, Hisuian Growlithe, Bloodmoon Ursaluna) also stay.

| Build (release) | ROM used | Free | Saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today (`hoenn` @ #93) | 32,711,444 | 823 KB | — |
| Gens 4–9 off, **keep list below** (recommended) | 24,136,436 | 9,197 KB | 8,575,008 B (8.2 MB) |
| Gens 4–9 off, nothing kept | 23,774,564 | 9,550 KB | 8,936,880 B (8.5 MB) |

Both builds compiled and linked with no other change (not booted in the emulator yet). The savings are graphics, cries, learnsets, dex text and icons of ~975 species entries; the species *numbers* stay, so saves and tables don't shift.

### Why it's nearly free
**HnS barely uses those species already.** A scan of every map, script, wild table and trainer party in the HnS build found Gen 4+ families only in:
- the **Sinjoh Ruins** story (Arceus, plus the Regigigas, Regieleki and Regidrago rooms; Dialga/Palkia/Giratina through Arceus's event),
- the **Alola islands** (the four Tapus; Noibat and Noivern on Ula'ula, 2 trainer slots),
- our own Melemele house (a Rockruff and a Pikipek, part 11).

No HnS wild table and only those two trainers use any other Gen 4+ family. Part 33's living dex tool already treats the 77 Sinnoh-only species as out of scope (Bryan, Nov 27). So the cut matches what the game already is.

**Keep list** (measured above): Arceus, Dialga, Palkia, Giratina, Regigigas, Regieleki, Regidrago, the four Tapus, Noibat, Rockruff, Pikipek. Rockruff and Pikipek could be swapped for Gen 1–3 pets instead (saves a little more).

### What it costs
- **The randomizer's "all generations" option** (HnS challenge menu, GEN SCOPE row) would only have Gen 1–3 to draw from. Its default is already Gen 1–3.
- **Link trades with unmodified HnS** could bring in a species that has no data here. HnS doesn't support trading with other hacks anyway, but worth one line in the release notes.
- The Pokédex and the PC need a check that disabled entries are skipped (the HGSS dex code already calls `IsSpeciesEnabled`).
- Part 33's living dex tool should read the same switch, so it never asks for a disabled species.

### Recommendation
**Do it, first in Phase H** (part 41, size M: the config change, the keep list, one emulator pass through dex/PC/randomizer/Battle Frontier/trade). It turns 823 KB free into ~9 MB, which every idea below needs: the lanes are still spending space, and rival followers, redesigned gyms and new maps aren't cheap. Wait for part 33 (#102) to merge so the living dex tool changes once.

## 2. Berry spots in Hoenn

What the repo shows (static check; not run in the emulator):
- **88 Hoenn berry trees** on kept maps, all with their own tree IDs. Part 19 already moved the three that collided with HnS trees; a fresh scan finds **no shared IDs** left (123 trees in all, 35 HnS).
- **All are planted** by `EventScript_ResetAllBerries` at new game. The 8 soil patches on Routes 104, 118, 121 and 123 start empty, as in Emerald.
- **Growth runs everywhere**: HnS's clock calls `BerryTreeTimeUpdate` for every tree, Hoenn's included (Gen 3 growth and yield rates).
- **Watering:** the berry tree script only accepts the **Wailmer Pail**, which only the Route 104 flower shop gives (a Hoenn flag, so it works). HnS's Squirt Bottle is just the Sudowoodo item and can't water. Watering only raises yield, so Johto trees still grow; they just give fewer berries before Hoenn.

**Recommendation:** small (S), fold into part 41 or the swarm part:
1. Emulator check: pick a Hoenn tree, plant in a soil patch, water, advance the clock, harvest.
2. Let the Squirt Bottle water too (one `checkitem` line), so Johto players can water before Hoenn. Optional.

## 3. Swarms

### What happened
- **HnS has no HGSS swarm system** (no daily swarm, no Mom/radio announcement). The only swarm code is Emerald's **TV mass outbreak** (`src/tv.c`).
- It runs in HnS: once `FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR` is set (HnS's champion), each `TryPutPokemonTodayOnAir` call has a 1-in-200 chance to start an outbreak show; watching it starts the outbreak for a day.
- **It points at the wrong maps.** It stores map group 0 + Emerald's map number. In the HnS build, group 0 is HnS's towns and routes, so the five Emerald outbreaks land on Johto/Kanto maps (found by reading the code; not emulator-checked):

| Emerald outbreak | Lands on (HnS group 0, same number) |
|---|---|
| Seedot Lv 3, Route 102 | Route 35 |
| Nuzleaf Lv 15, Route 114 | Route 47 |
| Seedot Lv 13, Route 117 | Viridian City |
| Seedot Lv 25, Route 120 | Vermilion City |
| Skitty Lv 8, Route 116 | Pallet Town |

  The show names the map from its header, so it would really say "Route 35". This is upstream HnS behavior, not something the Hoenn addition caused, but Hoenn's Lv 35–70 bands make Lv 3 swarms look even stranger.

### Recommendation: rework (part 42, M)
- Replace Emerald's five-entry list with a **Hoenn & Seek swarm table**: map (group + number, any region), species, level from the area's band, a rate. One swarm a day, rolled with the daily reset (HGSS style), **announced** on the TV news (part 13's hook) and by a forecast-style line on the PokéGear radio or a hint NPC.
- **Swarm-only species: yes, a few.** Good candidates are species the game has data for but no source: **Galarian forms of Gen 1–3** (Ponyta, Slowpoke, Farfetch'd, Meowth, Mr. Mime, Corsola, Zigzagoon, Weezing, the Galar birds are better as events), and some Alolan/Hisuian forms not already on the islands. They stay in the build after the cut (they're Gen 1–3 species). A daily rotation means every one comes up within a couple of weeks; the living dex tool would count the swarm table as a source.
- Also a few HGSS-flavored swarms of normal species (Yanma, Dunsparce, Qwilfish, Snubbull, Marill in Johto; Hoenn ones like Skitty, Seedot, Spinda at its band level).
- **Conflict:** part 33 (lane B) owns distribution. The swarm table must go through its living dex tool, so this waits for #102.

## 4. The rival (Silver)

### Where he is today
HnS's Silver has 7 teams per starter. The late ones: **Victory Road** (Lv 45–48), **Mt. Moon** (Lv 64) and a **daily rematch at the Indigo Plateau Pokémon Center** (fixed Lv 68: Bloodmoon Ursaluna, Crobat, Victreebel/Octillery/…, Houndoom, his starter, Tyranitar; 31 IVs, Basic AI). The player's cap passes 68 at the first Kanto badges and reaches 84–85 in Hoenn, so Silver stops mattering halfway through.

### Proposal (part 43, M; part 44, L)
**Scaling (43).** Use the same ladder as DAMIAN (part 16): his ace = the player's **progress cap**, worked out as if LEVEL CAP were on (`HoennGetPostJohtoLevelCap(…, FALSE)`), whatever the challenge setting. Cap it at **Brendan & May's level (ace 87)**. Gaps inside the team stay. Applies to the Indigo rematch (and Mt. Moon if you meet him late).

**Other ways his team gets stronger**, unlocked at milestones (Kanto champion, 4 Hoenn badges, Wallace, Brendan & May), each a new team tier:
1. Held items on every Pokémon (Life Orb, Choice Scarf, Leftovers, a berry), not just Full Restores.
2. Real movesets: coverage and setup moves instead of HnS's mid-game sets.
3. EVs (HnS's Red-style spreads) on top of his 31 IVs.
4. **Smart AI** from the second tier on (as on Red).
5. Roster growth that fits HGSS: Sneasel → Weavile, Magnemite → Magnezone, Gastly line, and one **Hoenn catch** after you've been there (he followed you; e.g. Bagon line or Beldum line).
6. One Hoenn appearance (e.g. at the Ever Grande Pokémon League after you win), so he's in the new region too, plus a PokéGear-free line from Brendan or May who ran into him.

**His starter following him (44).** Yes, feasible, and you're right that his scenes are scripted. Two ways:
- Duplicate every `applymovement` in his scenes for a second object (simple but ~15 scenes × 2 movement lists, easy to get out of step).
- **Recommended:** a small C helper: while a scene runs, the starter object steps onto the tile Silver just left (follower-style), so existing movement scripts stay untouched. The object's sprite is set per scene from `VAR_STARTER_MON` and his current stage (Chikorita → Bayleef → Meganium etc.).
- Where he stands still (Indigo, Mt. Moon), the starter stands beside or behind him like the gym followers (part 4b).
- Cost: a new C file + one hook line per scene (~15 HnS scenes). Large sprites are fine for NPC objects; HnS's 32×32 indoor rule only applies to the player's follower.

**Lane conflicts:** none directly; Silver's maps aren't lane maps. Part 35a (lane B) touches Cinnabar, part 34b (lane A) Route 41; neither has Silver.

## 6. Gym leaders' weekends and fan-favorite events

### Weekends off
**Pushback:** if a leader is away on weekends, a player who only plays on weekends can't progress. **Recommendation:** a leader takes weekends off **only after you've beaten that region's champion** (their rematch phase). Before that they're always in. On Saturdays and Sundays they and their follower are hidden in the gym (the gym guide says where they went) and appear at a weekend spot in the world with a line and their usual rematch.
- 24 leaders across Johto, Kanto and Hoenn; each needs a spot, a hide/show call (`GetDayOfWeek`, part 2) and a few lines. Hint lines say who's where.
- Size: one part per region (46–48, M each). Uses part 4b's followers (they share the leader's flag, so they move together).

### Fan-favorite chains (multi-week, fan service)
HnS already has HGSS's cameos to build on: **Misty** on Route 25 (the date scene), **Brock** at Diglett's Cave, **Blue** on Cinnabar. Proposal: one chain each for **Brock, Misty and Blue**, a step per weekend over 3–4 weeks (weekly flags from part 2), each step a short scene somewhere fitting (Brock: Mt. Moon fossils, Pewter Museum, a breeding question at the Day Care; Misty: Cerulean Cape, Seafoam's currents, a water show; Blue: Viridian Gym, Daisy and Oak in Pallet, a sparring match at Cinnabar). Good additions if you want more: **Lance** (Dragon's Den), **Whitney** (Goldenrod), **Steven** (Hoenn).
- **Reward: unique secret base decorations.** Dolls are cheap (they use follower sprites; IDs 127–255 are free, 129 slots): e.g. Onix, Starmie, Pidgeot dolls. A unique poster or mat per character needs new art, which costs a sitting each.
- Size: one part per character (49–51, M each).
- **Conflict:** Kanto maps belong to lane B until the lanes finish; these come after.

### Deeper conversations
Feasible and cheap (text only, ~1–2 KB per character). The hint engine (part 7) already picks a line by conditions on flags and vars. Each fan favorite gets a pool of 15–25 lines drawn from the games and the show, unlocked by what you've done (Misty notices your Psyduck following you; Brock asks about your Hoenn fossils; Blue knows you beat Red). Fold each pool into that character's chain part.

## 8. More Pokémon in towns and buildings

HnS already has **1,561 Pokémon objects** on its own maps, but almost all sit in scenery, and only **66 of 241 HnS interiors** have one. Proposal: a generator pass that gives people their Pokémon:
- **Owner–pet pairs** in towns: next to an NPC, on a walkable tile, facing them or wandering one tile; species fits the person (fisherman → Poliwag, old lady → Meowth, kid → Pichu) or the local wild table.
- **Houses and shops:** one pet per interior that has none (running the cry script like part 8's house pets).
- Rules: never block a path or door (the reachability flood fill from part 20a), at most a set number of objects per screen.
- Size: 56 (generator + Johto/Kanto, M), 57 (Hoenn, S).

## 9. Hoenn's serious trainers

Today (part 14): 44 trainers are **strong** (Cooltrainers, Experts, Dragon Tamers…, team at the stamp curve − 6) and 48 **hopeful** (curve − 14); teams are Emerald's, fully evolved.

**Bryan's idea:** the ones who know you and think they can win become gym-leader strength or tougher (no type theme), at or just below the next leader's level, scaled by badges.

**I like it, with one adjustment.** Making all 44 gym-leader strength turns every Hoenn route into a string of gym fights and makes the gyms feel smaller. Recommendation:
- **~15 "elite" trainers** (the Cooltrainers, Experts and Dragon Tamers who talk to you by name): **6 Pokémon, ace = next leader's ace − 1**, curated sets with held items, Smart AI. These are the gym-level fights.
- **The other strong trainers:** ace − 3 (from − 6), 4–5 Pokémon, better moves.
- Hopeful unchanged.
- Movesets: write them by hand, or start from Pokémon Showdown's Random Battle sets (MIT, section 11) and adjust.
- Size: 58 (rules + scaling, S), 59 (15 elite teams, M). Touches `trainer_curation.json` and `gen_hoenn_ids.py`, nothing lane-owned.
