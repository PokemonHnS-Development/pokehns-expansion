# Hoenn & Seek 2.0: Claude's pre-pass (parts 39a–b)

Done on `hoenn` after part 38 (PR #118), Nov 28, before Bryan's playtest (part 40). Every 2.0 part was already played in the emulator by its builder and again by the reviewer (`review-notes.md`); this pass looks for what falls between parts: real entrances, the release build, battles that were gated but never started, and cross-part interactions.

## What was checked

**Every new map is reachable from its real way in.** A flood fill over each map's walkable and surfable tiles, starting from its warps, map connections and every script warp into it, reaches an adjacent tile for every event object and sign, and the tile of every trigger. All 39 `*_hoenn` maps pass. The flags were all expected:
- the Lilycove lobby's receptionists, talked to across the counter, as in HnS's lobby
- the Melemele surf shop's clerk, served across the counter from (7,9) (played)
- the trench door's trigger, which ON_LOAD opens once the door is unlocked
- spare trigger tiles under trees in the meadow and the garden; the paths in still cross the same rows (checked)

**The release build plays the opening.** A new game on `pokehns-release.gba`:
- boots, then shows the title, NEW GAME, name entry and HnS's challenge settings
- reaches your bedroom
- plays the clock scene at the stairs and asks "What day is it today?" (the calendar is set)
- shows the tip window, then the wall clock offers to change the day

Every other test used the debug build.

**Legendary battles that were gated but never started:**
- JIRACHI starts at Lv 50 after three wishes and Monday night's white rock (you surf to it in the pond).
- The AURORA TICKET reaches BIRTH ISLAND from Vermilion's port: the menu's third entry.
- ARCEUS's catchable battle starts once the inscription is read (part 37 review).
- The Regi battles, Lugia and Deoxys themselves are HnS's own and unchanged.

**Cross-part checks (part 38):**
- the night weekday on three days at 1 AM and 10 PM
- the hint lines taking turns (Mahogany's hiker)
- calls that came due together ringing about 260 steps apart
- the refurnished florist and dance-teacher rooms
- the Vermilion smoke
- the text audit (no overflow, no one-word last lines)

**Budgets** (review item 54):
- release ROM 743 KB free
- about half of 2.0's flag blocks free
- no map over 64 objects
- the four roamer slots: Raikou, Entei, one Lati and Absol (Absol's stays reserved until it's caught)

## Not checked (needs Bryan, part 40)
- **A link trade and a link battle** between two mGBA windows on the same build: the harness runs one emulator.
- Anything that needs real time across days without the debug clock: daily berries, weekly chains, three Deoxys nights, three Ampharos nights.
- How it all feels in order on a real save: the event density, which calls arrive when, and whether hints are discoverable.
- The choices waiting on Bryan's eye: review items 1, 27 and 31 (choices), 26 (the faint sand hollow) and 15 (the Squirtle leader's sunglasses need sprite art).

## For the playtest
- Use the **release** patch for the playthrough. Use the **debug** patch (R + Start opens the debug menu: warps, flags, the "H&S time & weather…" clock tools) to jump to a part's checklist items.
- `playtest-checklist.md` lists every untested item by part. The part 38 section and the "General" section are the cross-cutting ones.
- 1.0 saves load into 2.0 (save version 6 clears only the new area), but set the weekday at any Pokémon Center wall clock first: an upgraded save starts with an arbitrary weekday.
